# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that builds a good project:

- being precise and kind in technical disagreement;
- assuming good faith and asking before assigning motive;
- giving and accepting actionable feedback;
- crediting other people's work;
- focusing on what is best for the people who will integrate the SDK.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, insults, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or attention;
- publishing private information without permission;
- sustained disruption of discussions or reviews;
- dismissing a report of a problem because "it works on my machine";
- encouraging unauthorized access to systems or data.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not open a public issue about a conduct report.

Maintainers will review reports promptly and may:

1. ask for a correction and a commitment to change;
2. remove a comment or close a discussion;
3. issue a warning;
4. temporarily or permanently ban participation.

They will avoid conflicts of interest and will not disclose the reporter's identity without consent.
